Waypoint brand guide

TaglineEvery stop, the same truth.
01 · The idea

A waypoint is a fixed point on a route where you check where you really are. Every Waypoint screen is one: the place where the plan and what actually happened are checked against each other, for the dispatcher, the loader, the driver and the store. The brand should feel like the depot it serves: printed manifests, painted bay numbers, safety-yellow floor tape. Useful before it is pretty.

02 · Logo
Waypoint
Primary · clear space = half the tile
WaypointOn dock dark
App icon

The mark is two waypoints joined by the run. Hollow is where the truck starts, filled is where it arrived, dotted is the road still to drive. The same line language is used on every map in the product. Minimum size: 20 px for the mark, 96 px wide for the lockup.

03 · Please don't
Recoloured tile
Red, green and blue mean status. The logo is never a status.
Stretched
Keep the tile square and the mark upright.
Effects
No gradients, glows or shadows. Flat, like floor paint.
No tile on a map
On busy backgrounds the mark always sits on its yellow tile.
04 · Brand colour
Safety yellow#FACC15
The brand colour. Tile, highlights, the current step.
Ink#1B1A17
Wordmark, text, structure.
Paper#F2F0EA
Light ground. Manifests, screens in daylight.
Dock#141412
Dark ground for the loader tablet and night use.
Proportion: about 60% paper, 30% ink, 10% yellow. Yellow is rare so it always means "look here".
05 · Status colours are not brand colours
4 shortDeliveredChilledNext stop

Signal red, done green and cold blue belong to the product, where each one means exactly one thing. They never appear in the logo, on marketing or on printed covers, so the meaning never wears out.
