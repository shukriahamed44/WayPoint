Deliverable · the core tradeoff

One system, two working speeds

Dispatcher · Store managerExplain

Planning 200+ orders against 60 vehicles before a 16:00 cutoff. Dense tables, filters and ranked lists on a big screen, and every deferral carries a written reason, because someone will ask why.

Loader · DriverExecute

Seconds per carton at the dock, one hand and full sun on the road, no signal in the hills. One action per screen, large targets, and everything works offline and catches up later.

The one rule that holds everywhere

A decision made on one role's screen is readable on the next role's screen without a phone call. Same fact, same number, same words.

05:38 · LD4Loader flags 6 of 10at the dock
05:40 · outletKegalle is toldbefore the truck leaves
08:44 · DR3Driver sees it on the PODno argument at the door
10:14 · DP1Dispatch sees it handledno call needed

So the four roles share a visual language (type, colour meanings, the route line) but not a layout. The desktop roles get density because they explain decisions after the fact. The field roles get simplicity because they act under time and physical pressure.
