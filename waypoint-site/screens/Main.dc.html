A delivery system built for the run Waypoint actually makes, not the demo run.

One system for the four people who move an order from a Colombo depot to a store shelf: 120 outlets, 60 vehicles, two depots. It answers three problems: planning is fragmented, decisions don't propagate, and problems are discovered too late. We designed the two moments where that hurts most: a dock four cartons short, and a driver who loses signal on the climb to Kandy.
