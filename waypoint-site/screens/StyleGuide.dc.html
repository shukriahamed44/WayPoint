Signature details, with rules
Hazard tape appears only when the workflow is degraded: a shortfall, no signal.
The route line: solid is done, dotted is still to drive. Also the logo.
Perforated edge on anything that is a record: manifests, notices.
Two themes, chosen by place
Field lightDriver, dispatch, store. Readable in sunlight.
Dock darkLoader tablet. No glare under dock lamps at 04:30.
Targets: 44 px on desktop, 56 px on the phone, 64 px on the dock tablet. Text contrast 4.5:1 or better.
Writing
Plain words, sentence case. Buttons start with a verb: "Flag 4 short and keep loading". Times are always 24-hour HH:MM. Quantities say what they count: "6 of 10 cartons". Anything a store manager reads has no internal codes, and always says why.
