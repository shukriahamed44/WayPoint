Waypoint

Every stop, the same truth.

A delivery planning system for the four people who move an order from a Colombo depot to a store shelf, designed around the moments the run actually breaks.

01

The three problems we designed around

Today the run is held together by spreadsheets, printed run sheets and phone calls. The people who find out last are the ones who have to deal with it.

01

Planning is fragmented

Orders, vehicle capacity, constraints, and store requirements live across spreadsheets, calls, and dispatcher knowledge.

What it looks like todayKavindi knows which outlets can't take a truck after eight and which mall shuts its dock at one. The spreadsheet doesn't. It also doesn't know VEH031 is in the workshop.
02

Decisions don't propagate

When a load changes, the driver, store, loader, and dispatcher may not see the same operational state.

What it looks like todayDispatch changes the stop order at 04:10. Ruwan finds out when the driver argues with him at the truck, holding a printout that is already wrong.
03

Problems are discovered too late

Shortages, connectivity failures, delivery issues, and deferrals often surface at the point of delivery instead of where they can still be managed.

What it looks like todayFour cartons missing at the dock at 05:38 become an argument at the Kegalle store at 08:44, between two people who never had the goods.
02

How it works

One fact, every screen. A decision made on one role's screen can be read on the next role's screen, without a phone call.

01

Plan

Dispatcher · before the 16:00 cutoff

Orders close at 16:00. Waypoint fits them into 60 vehicles by weight, slots and cold chain. When trucks run short, a written fairness rule suggests who waits, and the dispatcher makes the call.

DP3DP4
02

Load

Loader · the dock from 04:30

Trucks are loaded last stop first from a plan that is never older than the last change. A missing carton is flagged at the dock with one tap, before the truck leaves.

LD2LD4
03

Deliver

Driver · on the road

One stop at a time. Proof of delivery is a signature, a photo and an item count on the phone, and it keeps working with no signal, sending by itself when the signal returns.

DR3DR5
04

Confirm

Store manager and dispatcher

The store checks what arrived against what it ordered. A real conflict is shown side by side and decided by a person, and every decision is kept in the log.
